# Light/Dark Wallpaper Pairs

Recommended pairings for `WallpaperEngineMac` automation:

| Theme | Light/Day | Dark/Night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City Pop Sample | `city-pop-a-long-vacation-upscaled-4k` | `city-pop-dark-4k-aesthetic-city-night` |

Use the library window's `Set Light/Day` and `Set Dark/Night` buttons, then choose either `Follow System Appearance` or `Follow Day/Night Schedule` from the menu bar `Automation` menu.
